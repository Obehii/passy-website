Sharing, connections and shared trips

  • Nothing is shared until you choose to share it. When you share a place or add something to a shared trip, the people you've chosen can see it.
  • On a shared trip, everyone can add plans, packing items, places, memories, photos and tickets. You can remove what you added; the trip's owner can also remove plans and packing items others added.
